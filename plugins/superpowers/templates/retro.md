# <title> retro

## Review

Written by the reviewer when the review passes: the final verify summary line, the fix rounds run and the findings acted on or dropped, one line each.

## What went well

One line per point, from the review, the implementers' reports and the git log.

## What went wrong or could improve

One line per point: what cost time or tokens, what was delivered differently than planned, and the change to the flow or code that would prevent it.

## Recommendations

Three lists, each "none" when empty:

- Discovered and not built: work the implementers found outside the agreed rung.
- Out-of-scope ideas: improvements beyond the plan worth a plan of their own.
- Impossible at planning time: facts only implementation could reveal, and what the plan-writer should ask next time.
